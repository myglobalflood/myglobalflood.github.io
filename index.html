001Purpose

From hillslopes to transboundary basins, we develop models that make the dynamics of water visible, explainable and actionable.

从坡面到跨境流域，以模型洞察水的变化，让风险可见、可解释、可应对。

002Research

Three scales.
One connected system.

01

High-performance flood modelling

流域高性能洪水模拟与评估

Advancing controllable hydrological–hydrodynamic models, including VIC-CAS and CaMa-Flood, for reliable large-basin simulation and assessment.

↗
02

Flood change & attribution

变化环境下洪水变化与归因

Combining observations and models to reveal how climate and environmental change reshape flood components, extremes and future risk.

↗
03

Multi-scale flood monitoring

复杂水文情势洪水多尺度监测

Translating high-performance models into operational monitoring systems—from river floods to rapidly evolving mountain hazards.
